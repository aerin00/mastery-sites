Operational Workflow

01. Preparation

CRITICAL
  • Cleanse: Remove all sunscreen (SPF) and makeup. Mineral pigments block light transmission.
  • Exfoliate (Optional): Mild enzyme exfoliation improves light penetration.
  • Dry Skin: Use on dry skin. Water droplets can reflect photons.

02. Activation

ACTIVE PHASE
  • Fit: Secure strap high on the head. Mask should sit flush against the face.
  • Hydrogel (Optional): Apply CurrentBody Hydrogel mask underneath to increase hydration/conductivity.
  • Eyes: Use provided goggles if sensitive to brightness. Keep eyes closed.

03. Post-Care

ABSORPTION
  • Immediate: Apply Hyaluronic Acid or Peptides while skin is perfused (increased blood flow).
  • Actives: Retinol/Tretinoin or Vitamin C can now be applied safely.
  • Moisturize: Seal with a heavy moisturizer to prevent transepidermal water loss.
