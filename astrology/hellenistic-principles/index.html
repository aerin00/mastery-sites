3. Structural & Timing Doctrines

The project utilizes the Whole Sign House system and specific Hellenistic timing techniques to analyze biography and fate.

Angular Houses

1, 4, 7, 10

Strength: +4 to +5

The "Pivots" (Kentra). Planets here are loud, active, and effective. They define the native's core life pillars.

Succedent Houses

2, 5, 8, 11

Strength: +2 to +3

Rise to support the angles. Moderate strength. Often associated with resources, children, and social groups.

Cadent Houses

3, 6, 9, 12

Strength: -5 to +1

Falling away from angles. Generally weak or purely mental/spiritual realms. The 6th and 12th are often difficult.

Note on Planetary Joys

This project also incorporates the scheme of Planetary Joys, where planets find resourcefulness in specific houses (e.g., Moon in 3rd, Mercury in 1st, Mars in 6th, etc.), distinct from sign dignity.

Annual Profections

The most fundamental timing technique. The Ascendant moves 1 sign per year (30 degrees).

  • Activates one house and its ruler as the "Lord of the Year".
  • Cyclical: Returns to the Ascendant every 12 years (Age 0, 12, 24, 36, 48...).
  • Used to drill down into specific yearly themes.

Zodiacal Releasing (Aphesis)

An advanced time-lord technique from Vettius Valens. divides life into chapters based on the Lots of Fortune and Spirit.

  • Level 1 (Major): Decades-long periods.
  • Level 2 (Sub-periods): Months/Years.
  • Loosening of the Bond: Occurs when the sub-period jumps to the opposite sign, often marking a significant pivot or release of pressure.

Aspect Doctrine & Moiety Orbs

Unlike modern astrology which often uses standardized orbs (e.g., 10° for everything), this project uses Moieties (half-orbs) specific to each planet. An aspect exists if the sum of the two planets' moieties covers the distance between them.
