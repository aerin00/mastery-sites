Microsoft 365 automation under a partially federated operating model

A practical reference for the modern Microsoft 365 work stack: SharePoint, OneDrive, Copilot Notebooks, Pages, Loop, Forms, Lists, Planner, Teams, Office agents, scheduled and event-driven Copilot automation, plus governed Power Platform and enterprise controls.

Operating model

The safest interpretation is a four-lane model. Native Microsoft 365/Copilot features are a distinct self-service lane and should not be confused with citizen-built Power Platform solutions.

BC Hydro citizen boundary: Power Apps and Power Automate citizen development is permitted only when created directly from SharePoint Online and kept within the SharePoint list/library context. Standalone Power Platform solutions, Dataverse, SQL, APIs, on-prem systems, other enterprise data sources, enterprise-wide apps, and unmanaged/personal environments require the federated governance path. This restriction governs citizen-built Power Platform solutions; it does not, by itself, prohibit normal use of native first-party tools such as Copilot Notebooks, Loop, Pages, Lists Forms, Microsoft Forms, Planner, or OneNote.

1. Citizen Power Platform

SharePoint-originated, SharePoint-scoped Power Apps forms and Power Automate flows under the stated citizen exception.

2. Native M365 / Copilot

Notebooks, Pages, Loop, Forms, Lists Forms, Planner, Search, Office agents, scheduled prompts, Cowork, OneNote, Whiteboard, and similar first-party work experiences.

3. Federated delivery

Cross-service flows/apps, Copilot Studio, premium connectors, transactional agents, Syntex, and broader business solutions.

4. Central platform / security

Tenant-wide Graph apps, identity, Purview, Defender, SharePoint Advanced Management, agent governance, and cross-tenant controls.

The highest-value design is not “automate everything with Power Automate.” Use each Microsoft 365 surface for the job it is best at, then keep the durable record in SharePoint.

Governance guardrails

These are the operating rules that matter most when deciding whether a technically possible automation is appropriate for self-service use.

ControlPractical rule
Citizen-development boundaryCreate Power Apps/flows only from SharePoint Online and keep them interacting only with the originating SharePoint list/library. Anything broader goes to IT Advisor/federated review.
No standalone Power PlatformStandalone Power Apps or Power Automate flows are outside the stated citizen exception.
No external systems for citizen buildsDataverse, SQL, APIs, on-prem systems and other enterprise data sources require the federated path.
No personal/unmanaged bypassDo not use personal or unmanaged Power Platform environments to bypass the operating model.
Least privilegeFlow connections, app identities, Graph scopes, and agent tools should have only the access needed for the approved process.
Human-in-the-loopUse approval or deterministic validation before consequential deletes, permission grants, disclosure, financial decisions, or security changes.
Content governance before CopilotCopilot respects existing permissions. Overshared SharePoint content should be remediated before broad AI rollout.
Agent governanceProduction agents need named owners, controlled sources/tools, an approved audience, authentication, DLP, and review dates.
Retention/classification is centralBusiness makers can use approved labels but should not invent retention periods or enterprise classification schemes.
External collaboration is centralGuest lifecycle, cross-tenant access, shared channels, sponsor/expiry, and Conditional Access remain IAM/security responsibilities.
