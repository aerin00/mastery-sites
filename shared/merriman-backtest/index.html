Merriman Claims: Backtested

Six published Merriman market-timing claims, operationalized and tested against daily OHLC history (1970–2026) with Astrolog 8.00 Swiss Ephemeris positions. Verdicts are statistical honesty, not advocacy.
Data: S&P 500 1970–2026, DJIA 1992–2026, gold / silver / WTI 2000–2026 (Yahoo), BTC 2011–2026 (Bitstamp); audited extracts in 05_Finance_Crypto/ohlc_history. Astronomy: Astrolog 8.00 (Swiss Ephemeris), daily 12:00 UT longitudes, Sun–Saturn, generated by gen_ephemeris.py. Statistics: two-sided permutation tests, 10,000 shuffles, seed 42; Bonferroni bar across 6 tests is p < 0.0083. Verdict key: supported = p < 0.05 in the claimed direction; not_supported = significant against; inconclusive = no significant effect (direction may still lean as claimed). Small-n claims are descriptive only. Gold and silver rows before 2012 carry disclosed source quirks. No position sizing, no forecasts, no advice.
